About this cake

Bring your idea to life with a bespoke themed cake handcrafted in Huntingdonshire. From Bluey & Bingo to superheroes, football clubs, gaming, florals and minimalist styles.

  • Designed for your occasion — no ready-made stock
  • Fresh bakes, Level 2 hygiene, allergen‑aware handling
  • Delivery across Huntingdon, Cambridge & Peterborough

Popular finishes

Smooth buttercream · palette knife texture · gentle drizzle · simple toppers/flowers.

Design directions

Clean & minimal · soft pastels · gold accents · theme hints (not full character cakes).

Lead time

Weekdays: usually 7–14 days. Weekends & busy dates — book earlier to secure your slot.

Care & transport

Carry flat; keep cool (not hot car). Best the same day/next day. I’ll provide storage tips on delivery.

Quick questions

before you order

Do you deliver?
Yes — I deliver across Cambridgeshire (Huntingdon, Cambridge, Peterborough). Pickup is also available.
How far in advance?
Weekdays: usually 7–14 days. For weekends and busy dates, please book as early as possible to secure your slot.
Allergens
I handle allergens carefully and can often suggest alternatives. Cakes are prepared in a kitchen that handles common allergens.
Storage
Carry flat; keep cool (not a hot car). Best the same day or next morning. I provide storage tips upon delivery.
