Cupcakes

Beautifully finished cupcake boxes created for gifts, parties, dessert tables and corporate occasions.

About this cake

Cupcakes are a polished way to serve individual portions without losing the feeling of a bespoke dessert. They work beautifully for birthday tables, office treats, baby showers, thank-you gifts, branded sets and weddings where guests can enjoy a coordinated little cake without cutting a large one.

Easy to serve

Individual portions make them practical for parties, offices, children’s events and dessert tables.

Coordinated designs

Colours, toppers, logos, names, ages or edible image details can be matched to your event or gift theme.

Mixed flavour boxes

Larger boxes can include more than one flavour, following the cupcake flavour guide below.

Do you deliver?
Yes — cupcake boxes can be delivered across Cambridgeshire or collected by arrangement, which works well for gifts, offices and event setup.
How far in advance?
Standard cupcake boxes usually need 4–7 days; larger event quantities or branded sets need more notice.
Can I choose more than one cupcake flavour?
Yes, depending on the box size. A box of 6 is one flavour, boxes of 12 or 18 can include up to two flavours, and boxes of 24 or more can include up to three flavours.
Can you add toppers or logos?
Yes — I can add names, numbers, edible images or simple branded toppers depending on the brief.

Popular cupcake extras

useful details for gift boxes and event sets

Decoration options

  • Single-colour or two-tone swirls, sprinkles and coordinated colour palettes across the full set.
  • Name toppers, age toppers, logo toppers or simple edible image details.
  • Mixed boxes can be kept neat and elegant or made brighter for party themes.
  • If you send a reference box or mood board, I can recreate the overall feel in my own style.

What to send me

  • Quantity, date and whether this is for gifting, an office order or a dessert table.
  • Preferred colours, event theme and any wording or topper details.
  • Logo or image file if you want branded toppers or edible prints.
  • Postcode so I can confirm delivery timing and quote.

Extra option: I can add a personalised printed card with your own image and/or text when the cupcake box is going straight to the recipient.
