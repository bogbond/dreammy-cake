About this cake

Treat your guests to handcrafted cupcakes made fresh in Huntingdon. Choose classic swirls, two-tone piping or palette-knife textures, with optional edible prints or acrylic toppers (names, numbers, logos). Boxes of 6 / 12 / 24 are perfect for birthdays, baby showers, corporate treats and dessert table

  • 5★ Food Hygiene Rating (Very Good)
  • Baked fresh to order (no ready-made stock)
  • Delivery across Huntingdon • Cambridge • Peterborough (free within 5 km)

🍰 Classic Buttercream

Buttercream: Vanilla, Milk Chocolate, White Chocolate, Lemon, Cookies & Cream, Nutella, Biscoff, Salted Caramel
Optional fillings: Jam, Lemon Curd, Biscoff, Nutella, Salted Caramel, Milk/White/Orange Chocolate Ganache

🍒 Cherry‑Chocolate

Moist chocolate sponge with a brownie‑like texture.

Whipped milk chocolate ganache, cherry-berry compote, milk‑chocolate mousse.

🍫 Total Chocolate

Rich chocolate sponge. Whipped milk‑chocolate ganache.

Creamy mousse with milk and dark chocolate.

🥜 Snickers

Moist chocolate sponge with mascarpone frosting and milk‑chocolate mousse.

Salted caramel with peanuts.

🍓 Strawberry Ice Cream

Vanilla sponge with creamy strawberry mousse and strawberry‑berry confit.

White‑chocolate ganache cream.

🎂 Red Velvet

Moist red‑velvet sponge with mascarpone frosting.

Cherry berry confit and creamy cherry mousse.

Prefer a different twist? You can request swaps (e.g. dark vs milk chocolate) — add this to the form and I’ll confirm.
Do you deliver?
Yes — I deliver across Cambridgeshire (Huntingdon, Cambridge, Peterborough). Pickup is also available.
How far in advance?
Weekdays: usually 7–14 days. For weekends and busy dates, please book as early as possible to secure your slot.
Allergens
I handle allergens carefully and can often suggest alternatives. Cakes are prepared in a kitchen that handles common allergens.
Storage
Carry flat; keep cool (not a hot car). Best the same day or next morning. I provide storage tips upon delivery.
