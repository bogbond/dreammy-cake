Mini / Bento Cake

Small personalised cakes created for thoughtful gifts, cosy dates and intimate celebrations.

About this cake

A mini / bento cake is a small, personal gift for moments that do not need a large cake but still deserve something beautiful. It is perfect for couples, best friends, thank-you gifts, cosy birthdays, date nights or a sweet surprise for two to four people.

A thoughtful little gift

Add a short message, name, age, colour palette, bow, ribbon or playful detail to make it feel made just for them.

Perfect for small moments

Ideal when you want a beautiful cake without ordering more servings than you need.

Gift-ready presentation

Works well in a clear box or lunchbox-style presentation for surprise deliveries and intimate celebrations.

Quick questions

before you order

Do you deliver?
Yes — mini / bento cakes are available for delivery across Cambridgeshire or collection by arrangement, which makes them especially easy to send as a gift.
How many people does a bento cake serve?
Most mini / bento cakes are best for two to four people, making them ideal for intimate gifts, date nights and small celebrations.
Is it good for gifting?
Absolutely — mini / bento cakes are one of my most popular gift options, especially in a clear box with ribbon and a short piped message.
Can I add a personalised card?
Yes — for an extra charge I can add a printed card with your own image and/or message, which pairs perfectly with the gift-box style presentation.

What works best on a mini / bento cake

small format, but still plenty of room to personalise

Popular decoration options

  • Short piped wording, mini toppers, ribbons, bows, hearts, pearls and vintage borders.
  • A small edible photo or printed detail can work well when the layout stays simple.
  • You can choose a lunchbox style or a clear gift box depending on the look you want.
  • If you send a reference cake, I can adapt it to a smaller format so it still looks balanced.

What to send me

  • Your date and whether you prefer a lunchbox or a clear gift-box style.
  • The message you want on top, plus any colours or finish you like.
  • Box style preference if you already know it.
  • One to three inspiration photos if there is a specific look you want me to follow.

Extra option: For an extra charge I can add a personalised printed card with your own image and/or text. Mini cakes work best with short wording rather than lots of tiny design elements.
