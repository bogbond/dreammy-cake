Father’s Day Cupcake & Brownie Boxes

A thoughtful Father’s Day collection of personalised cupcakes and rich brownies, beautifully presented for gifting.

Father’s Day pre-orders are open 👔

A gift-ready Father’s Day collection with cupcake boxes, a Best Dad Ever brownie option, and soft blue details made for a thoughtful celebration.

Choose a cupcake box, brownie, or both, add an optional greeting card, then I’ll confirm your slot and payment details by email.

Why Dreamy Cake Bakery

  • Made to order for Father’s Day gifting
  • Cupcake box £18, brownie £25, or both £43
  • Slot secured after confirmation and full payment
Prefer WhatsApp? Send your product choice, quantity, collection/delivery option and postcode — I’ll reply with availability and payment details.
WhatsApp
When do Father’s Day pre-orders close?
Slots are limited and close once the available 20 and 21 June collection or delivery times are full. Your slot is secured only after I confirm availability and full payment is received.
What can I order?
Choose the Father’s Day Cupcake Box (£18), the Best Dad Ever Brownie (£25), or both (£43 before any greeting card or paid delivery quote).
Can I choose cupcake flavours?
Yes — cupcake flavours are Vanilla with jam or Chocolate. If you choose the brownie only, cupcake flavour is not required.
How does collection or delivery work?
Collection is from Brampton on 20 June morning or 21 June morning. Free local delivery is available for Brampton, Huntingdon and Godmanchester; other areas can request a paid delivery quote.
Is payment required to secure my order?
Yes — I will confirm availability first, then your order slot is held only after full payment is received.
