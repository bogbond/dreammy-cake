Express Cupcakes — fast turnaround

Need cupcakes fast? Express Cupcakes are a simple, gift‑ready cupcake box from our Express Menu — available today or tomorrow (subject to availability). Choose your box size, flavour and frosting, then add optional extras if you’d like.

Available box sizes: 6 / 12 / 18 / 24 / 36 / 48 cupcakes (no intermediate sizes). We’ll confirm your slot and the final total after your request.

Why Dreamy Cake Bakery

  • Made to order — every box is personalised for your occasion
  • Photo-ready finish — clean, delicate details (perfect for gifting)
  • Collection / local delivery around Huntingdon & nearby areas

How fast can I get Express Cupcakes?
Usually today or tomorrow (subject to availability). Express slots are limited — I’ll reply to confirm your slot after your request.
Which box sizes are available?
Fixed box sizes only: 6 / 12 / 18 / 24 / 36 / 48 cupcakes (no intermediate sizes).
Which frosting types can I choose?
Buttercream or Cream cheese. Your frosting choice doesn’t change the base price.
Can I add an edible print, and how is it priced?
Yes. Edible print is priced per 6 cupcakes: +£2 per 6 (e.g. +£4 for 12, +£8 for 24). Add details or a link in the “Edible print details” field.
Can I add sprinkles, and how is it priced?
Yes. Sprinkles are priced per 6 cupcakes: +£1 per 6 (e.g. +£2 for 12, +£4 for 24).
Do you deliver, and when is the delivery fee confirmed?
Yes — choose Delivery and add your postcode/area. Delivery fee is confirmed separately when I confirm your express slot (not included in the estimate).
