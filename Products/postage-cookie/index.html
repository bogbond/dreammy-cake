Fondant cookie

Elegant fondant cookies - perfect as wedding favours, baby showers, birthdays and brand events. I bake a smooth vanilla sugar cookie and finish it with thin layer of ready-to-roll fondant, embossed printed to match your theme (names, dates, monograms, logos). Each cookie can be individually heat-sealed and ribboned for gifting, with optional custom tags.

  • Designed for your occasion — no ready-made stock
  • Fresh bakes, Level 2 hygiene, allergen‑aware handling
  • Postage UK‑wide (Royal Mail) + local pickup in Huntingdon

Popular finishes

Smooth fondant tops · clean embossed/debossed names, dates, monograms or logos · soft colour palettes · subtle gold/silver details.

Design directions

Keep it minimal and elegant. One–two colours, clear lettering and simple shapes work best on cookies sent by post.

Lead time

Usually 7–14 days. For larger quantities or popular dates, please enquire earlier to secure your slot.

Care & transport

Letterbox‑friendly packaging. Store in a cool, dry place (not the fridge). Best within 7–10 days. Each cookie can be heat‑sealed; ribbons/tags optional.
