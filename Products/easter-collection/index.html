Can I order the Easter collection outside the seasonal promo window?
Yes — these designs can be requested year-round. The collection is promoted more actively closer to Easter, but early enquiries are welcome any time.
Can I mix products and quantities?
Yes — use the plus and minus buttons in the order builder to mix Easter cakes and cupcakes. The estimate updates automatically and bulk discounts apply from 4 items.
How do Easter discounts work?
Bulk discounts apply automatically in the estimate: 4 items get 10% off, 5 items get 15% off, 6 items get 20% off, and 8 or more items get 25% off. Final price is confirmed by email.
Can I leave a short note with my order?
Yes — use the “Extra Easter notes or short message” field for a greeting, delivery note, or anything else you would like me to know.
