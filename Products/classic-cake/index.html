Classic Cake — Express menu

Need a cake fast? Classic Cake is a clean, premium‑finish cake from our Express menu — available today or tomorrow (subject to availability). Choose your size and 1 of 2 styles, then send your request. I’ll reply to confirm your slot and the final total.

Sizes
  • 6 inch — £60 — 8–12 servings
  • 8 inch — £80 — 14–20 servings
Styles
  • Chocolate & Biscoff Drip Cake — flavour inside: Choco Biscoff
  • White & Gold Teddy Cake — flavour inside: Vanilla Raspberry

Express slots are limited — I’ll confirm your slot after your request. Delivery fee is confirmed separately.

How fast can I order a Classic Cake from the express menu?
Usually for today or tomorrow (subject to availability). Express slots are limited — I’ll reply to confirm your slot after your request.
What sizes are available and how many servings?
Two sizes: 6 inch (£60, 8–12 servings) or 8 inch (£80, 14–20 servings).
What’s the difference between the two styles?
Chocolate & Biscoff Drip Cake has a rich drip finish; White & Gold Teddy Cake has a clean white‑and‑gold teddy design. The base price depends only on the size.
Can I choose the flavour?
For the express menu, the flavour is fixed based on your chosen style: Choco Biscoff inside for the Drip Cake, or Vanilla Raspberry inside for the Teddy Cake.
How does the topper work?
Optional personalised topper on a stick (+£4.50). Add your topper text (up to 25 characters). Colour is chosen by the baker to match your cake.
How does delivery work?
Choose Collection or Delivery and add your postcode/area. Delivery fee is confirmed separately when I confirm your slot (not included in the estimate).
When is payment required?
After I confirm availability and the total (including any delivery), I’ll send payment details. Your slot is reserved once paid.
