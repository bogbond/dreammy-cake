Express Bento Cake — fast turnaround

Need a cake fast? Express Bento Cake is a clean buttercream bento — available today or tomorrow (subject to availability). Choose your flavour and cake colour, then add simple extras if you’d like.

Perfect for last‑minute gifts and small celebrations: 2–4 servings, quick confirmation, and a premium finish.

Why Dreamy Cake Bakery

  • Made to order — every box is personalised for your occasion
  • Photo-ready finish — clean, delicate details (perfect for gifting)
  • Collection / local delivery around Huntingdon & nearby areas
Prefer WhatsApp? Send your date + servings + postcode + style ideas — I’ll reply with availability and a quote.

How fast can I get an express bento?
Usually today or tomorrow. Slots are limited — I’ll confirm availability within 59 minutes during working hours.
What’s included in the £30 base price?
£30 includes a 2–4 serving bento cake, one flavour, white cake colour, and a clean buttercream finish. Optional extras can be added.
What flavours are available?
Chocolate or Vanilla.
What cake colours can I choose?
White is included. Pink, Blue or Purple are +£2, and Red is +£3.
How long can the Lay-flat cake topper text be?
Up to 25 characters.
Can I choose the topper or bow colour?
To keep the express order quick, you only choose the cake colour. I’ll match the Lay-flat cake topper and bow to your cake/design so it looks cohesive.
How does delivery work / when is delivery fee confirmed?
Choose Delivery and add your postcode/area. I’ll confirm the delivery fee when I confirm your slot (delivery isn’t included in the estimate).
When is payment required?
After I confirm availability and the total (including any delivery), I’ll send payment details. Your slot is reserved once paid.
