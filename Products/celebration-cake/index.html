Celebration Cake

Bespoke celebration cakes designed for birthdays, anniversaries and meaningful family moments.

About this cake

A celebration cake is a refined centrepiece for birthdays, anniversaries and family gatherings — elegant enough for the table, but still personal to the person you are celebrating. The design can be led by favourite colours, flowers, textures, flavours, initials or subtle details without becoming a full novelty theme.

A refined centrepiece

Made to look polished when it arrives at the table, with a finish that feels special, thoughtful and photo-ready.

Personal, not overdone

Colours, florals, bows, pearls, initials, age, favourite flavours and small meaningful details can be added with an elegant finish.

Made for milestone moments

A strong choice for parents, partners, adult birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers and family celebrations.

Quick questions

before you order

Do you deliver?
Yes — I deliver celebration cakes across Cambridgeshire or you can collect by arrangement. I’ll confirm timing once your design, date and postcode are approved.
How far in advance?
Most celebration cakes are best booked 7–10 days ahead. Baby showers, milestone birthdays and Saturdays tend to fill first.
Can the cake feel personal without being themed?
Yes — celebration cakes can include favourite colours, florals, bows, initials, age details or favourite flavours while still keeping a refined, elegant look.
Can I add a personalised card?
Yes — for an extra charge I can add a printed card with your own image and/or message, which is perfect for gifting or surprise delivery.

Ways to personalise a celebration cake

useful options to mention in your enquiry

Decoration options

  • Vertical toppers on picks or flat toppers and plaques in gold, silver, black or colour-matched finishes.
  • Fresh or dried flowers, bows, pearls, butterflies and vintage piping all work beautifully on this style.
  • If you send a reference cake, I can recreate the overall look in my own style and adapt it to your size and budget.
  • An edible photo or printed detail can be added when it suits the design.

What to send me

  • Your event date and roughly how many people you want to serve.
  • Preferred colours and overall style: clean, vintage, floral, bow, butterfly and so on.
  • Any wording for the topper, board or front of the cake.
  • One to three reference photos if you already have a look in mind.

Extra option: For an extra charge I can add a personalised printed card with your own image and/or text.
