Wedding Cakes

Bespoke wedding cakes and coordinated desserts designed to complement the style, scale and atmosphere of your celebration.

About this cake

A wedding cake should feel like part of the whole celebration, not just a dessert. I plan each cake around your venue, guest count, colour palette, florals, styling and the atmosphere of the day — from elegant single-tier cakes to dramatic multi-tier centrepieces, matching cupcakes and dessert-table details.

  • Bespoke design support, from size and servings to colour, flavour, flowers, structure and display styling
  • Statement cakes, tiered designs, surprise details and matching cupcakes created to feel cohesive with your wedding
  • Professional delivery and on-site setup available for cakes that need safe transport, assembly or final decoration at the venue

Wedding cake service:

You are not just ordering sponge and buttercream. The service includes design guidance, flavour planning, guest-count advice and practical support so the cake fits the style, scale and flow of your day.

Venue delivery & on-site setup:

Smaller wedding cakes may be suitable for collection or boxed delivery. Taller, tiered or more delicate designs may need professional delivery, separate transport of tiers and final assembly at the venue to protect the structure, freshness and finish.

Matching desserts:

Cupcakes or small desserts can be designed in the same colours and details as the main cake, creating a more complete dessert table for guests and photos.

What to send when you enquire:

Please include your date, guest count, venue or postcode, preferred style, colour palette, flower plans and any inspiration photos.

Quick questions

wedding planning essentials

Do you provide a full wedding cake service?
Yes — I can help with design direction, flavour planning, guest-count guidance, delivery coordination and on-site setup where the cake design requires it.
Do you deliver and set up wedding cakes at the venue?
Yes — venue delivery is available by arrangement, and tiered cakes can be assembled and finished on site where needed. Please include the venue name, postcode and preferred delivery window in your enquiry.
Can we add matching cupcakes or desserts?
Yes — matching cupcakes or small desserts can be designed around the same colours, styling and decorative details as the wedding cake.
