    Flavour guide

    Cake Flavour Menu

    Our cakes are made with soft sponge layers, smooth creams and carefully chosen fillings. Classic flavours are included in the base cake price, while Premium flavours are available for an additional charge depending on cake size.

    Classic FlavoursIncluded in the base cake price.

    Vanilla Classic

    Soft vanilla sponge layered with smooth vanilla buttercream. Raspberry or strawberry compote can be added at no extra cost.

    Double Chocolate

    Rich chocolate sponge layered with smooth chocolate buttercream.

    Red Velvet

    Velvety buttermilk sponge with a hint of cocoa, layered with smooth cream cheese frosting.

    Classic Lemon

    Zesty lemon sponge layered with lemon buttercream and lemon curd.

    Premium FlavoursAvailable from +£5 depending on cake size.

    Strawberry Ice Cream

    Soft vanilla sponge layered with creamy strawberry mousse, strawberry-berry confit and smooth white chocolate mascarpone cream.

    Biscoff Caramel

    Soft brown sugar sponge layered with Biscoff buttercream, Biscoff spread and biscuit crumb.

    Chocolate Dulce de Leche

    Rich chocolate sponge layered with silky chocolate ganache, chocolate cream and dulce de leche.

    Pistachio Raspberry

    Soft pistachio sponge layered with raspberry compote and silky pistachio cream cheese frosting.

    Lemon Elderflower Blueberry

    Zesty lemon sponge layered with elderflower mascarpone cream, lemon curd and blueberry compote.

    Cookies & Cream

    Marbled vanilla and chocolate sponge layered with Oreo buttercream and crushed Oreo pieces.
