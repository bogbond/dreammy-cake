Meet Margo

Founder & baker at Dreamy Cake

Stylish designs. Memory‑worthy taste.

Hello! I’m Margo — a self‑taught baker who turned a lifelong hobby into Dreamy Cake. I design and bake bespoke cakes and cupcakes that feel personal and look clean and modern. Every order is made to measure: from flavour to finish, you get a cake that truly fits the moment.

  • Made to order — no stock cakes
  • Level 2 Food Hygiene certified
  • Allergen‑aware handling
  • Serving Huntingdon, Cambridge, Peterborough
