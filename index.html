FAQ — quick answers

Top questions answered at a glance.

Is my date available?
We typically need 7–14 days. Rush orders may be possible by request. Tell us your date and delivery postcode — we’ll confirm availability and hold your slot with a 50% deposit. Usual reply time: 2–3 hours.
How much will my cake cost?
Prices vary by servings, design detail and delivery. Check our Cake Menu, and a servings guide is available on each product page; send your inspo, and we’ll suggest the right size with a no‑obligation quote in 2–3 hours.
What size do I need?
Use the servings guide on each product page to match guest count ↔ cake size. If you’re between sizes, we’ll recommend the most cost‑efficient option and adapt the design to suit.
Do you deliver and set up?
Yes — we deliver within ~50 km of Cambridgeshire; further afield on request. Fees are postcode‑based. For tiered/tall designs we can handle venue setup for safe placement. See Delivery for details.
Can you do vegan / gluten‑friendly / nut‑free?
Options available on request. However, all cakes are made in a kitchen that handles gluten, dairy, eggs, nuts and soy, so they may not be suitable for severe allergies. See our Allergens page.
