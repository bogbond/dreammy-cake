Frequently Asked Questions

All the essentials — ordering, delivery, pricing, and lead times.

Quick answers on ordering, lead times, delivery areas (Huntingdon, Cambridge, Peterborough), pricing, sizes, flavours and allergens. Use the filters or open all.

Where can I collect my order?

Our bakery is located in Brampton, Huntingdon, about a 30-minute drive from Cambridge. The exact address will be provided once your order is confirmed.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we offer delivery to Huntingdon, Cambridge, Peterborough, and nearby towns and villages. For some orders, we may be able to arrange delivery to London as well.

Do you offer free delivery?

We offer free delivery within a 5-mile radius of Huntingdon. For locations beyond that, delivery charges will be calculated specifically for your order.

Is weekend or early morning/late evening delivery available?

Yes, we can accommodate deliveries on weekends or at specific times, but this should be arranged when placing your order, as there may be certain limitations.

Can I request custom decorations or personalized text on my order?

Absolutely! We’re happy to customize your cake or cupcakes with decorations, names, and more. Once you submit the order form, we’ll discuss the specifics with you.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, in some cases, a deposit of 50% of the order value is required. Please refer to our refund/cancellation policy for more details.

Can I get my deposit back if I cancel my order?

Yes, as long as you cancel at least 10 days before the scheduled delivery date. More details are available in our refund/cancellation policy.

What is the preferred way to contact you?

Email is our preferred method of communication, as it is secure and keeps all details in one place for easy reference.

What happens after I submit my cake order form?

Once you submit your order form, we’ll reach out to you within 1-2 business days. Due to high demand, we may not be able to fulfill every request. If your order can proceed, you’ll receive a detailed quote by email. After you approve the quote, an invoice will be sent, and payment will be required within 48 hours to confirm the order. Please check your spam folder in case our email goes there!

How far in advance should I place my order?

We recommend placing orders 2-3 weeks in advance. For urgent orders, we do offer an expedited service with an additional charge. Please specify any urgent needs in the order form.

Can I make changes to my order after it has been submitted?

Yes, you can make changes to your order after it has been fully agreed upon, but this must be done at least 10 days before your scheduled delivery date. If you need to make changes after this deadline, additional charges may apply.

How should I store my cake after receiving it?

To keep your cake fresh, we recommend storing it in the refrigerator at a temperature between 0°C and 5°C (32°F to 41°F). Our cakes are made without preservatives, so they are best enjoyed within 7–14 days after delivery.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash or bank transfer as payment methods for your orders.

How far in advance should I book?

Most dates fill 2–3 weeks ahead; peak weekends and tiered cakes fill earlier. Tell us your event date — we’ll confirm realistic options. Please specify any urgent needs in the order form.

How do I place an order?

Send the order form with date, servings, flavour and a brief idea. Or start directly on a product page: Celebration Cake, Themed Cake, Mini/Bento, Cupcakes. We reply within 1–2 business days with availability and a quote. Your invoice is due within 48 hours to secure the slot.

Do you accept rush orders?

Sometimes, if the schedule allows. A rush fee may apply for priority prep/materials; we’ll confirm feasibility first.

Can I reserve a date without details yet?

Yes — we can hold a date after a deposit. Final design details are due 10 days before your event.

What info helps you quote accurately?

Date/time, servings, flavour, inspiration photos/colours, venue or collection, any allergies, and a budget range.

Do you do tasting boxes?

Occasionally (limited dates). Email dreamycake.info@gmail.com with your event date, preferred flavours and collection/delivery area, and we’ll confirm the next available slot and options.

Can I order via Instagram/DM?

For clarity and records, we finalise orders by email/form. We’re happy to answer quick questions in DMs, then move to email to book.

How much do your cakes cost?

Pricing depends on size, flavour and design time. See current “from” prices in the Pricing section.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes — a 50% deposit secures your date (non‑refundable). The balance is due before collection/delivery.

Are personalised cakes refundable if I change my mind?

Under UK rules, bespoke/personalised goods don’t carry a no‑fault right to cancel (the 14‑day cooling‑off for distance sales doesn’t apply). Faulty or not‑as‑described items remain protected by consumer law. See gov.uk for details.

What counts as “faulty or not as described”?

We follow UK consumer standards; if an item is faulty or not as described, we’ll assess evidence and resolve per law. Keep the cake cool/flat and send photos promptly so we can help.

Can I split payment?

Usually yes (deposit + balance). The booking is confirmed only after the deposit is received.

Do you charge for design revisions?

Your quote includes one minor revision. Extra concept work may be billed.

Do you deliver? Where to?

Local delivery across Huntingdon, Cambridge, Peterborough, St Ives, St Neots, Ely and nearby villages; some London deliveries by arrangement. See Delivery details. Delivery is quoted by distance and time window.

Can I collect?

Yes — collection from Brampton, Huntingdon (PE28) at an agreed time. We share the exact address after confirmation.

How do delivery time windows work?

We give a window based on route and traffic. Early‑morning/late‑evening slots are possible if pre‑arranged.

What if I miss the delivery window?

We’ll try to re‑route where possible; additional fees may apply. If same‑day re‑delivery isn’t feasible, collection can be arranged.

Venue setup for tiered cakes?

Available on request (fees depend on distance, time and access). Please share venue contact and loading details.

Who is responsible after handover?

After collection/delivery, care and transport are the customer’s responsibility; we photograph items before handover as condition proof.

Do you offer nationwide shipping?

Fresh cakes are hand‑delivered or collected only. Postable items (e.g., cookie favours) may be offered seasonally.

Can I change delivery address/time?

Usually possible up to 48 hours before; later changes depend on route and may incur a fee.

What cake size do I need?

Tell us headcount and slice style (party ≈ 1×2×5 cm; finger ≈ 2.5×2.5×10 cm). Guide for tall single tiers:
6" (15 cm): ~10–12 party / ~20 finger
8" (20 cm): ~20–24 party / ~40 finger
10" (25 cm): ~35–40 party / ~60–70 finger. Servings vary by height and cutting method; wedding charts often assume smaller slices.

Do you make tiered cakes?

Yes. We’ll plan boards/dowels and advise the right stand and transport approach.

Can tiers be different flavours?

Yes — each tier can be different. For a single tier, one flavour keeps structure consistent.

Do you provide cutting guides?

Yes — on request, we include a quick diagram with your order confirmation.

What flavours do you offer?

Popular picks: Classic Vanilla, Cherry‑Chocolate, Total Chocolate, Snickers, Strawberry Ice Cream, Red Velvet. We’ll match fillings to travel/storage needs.

Do you use fresh cream?

We can, but for hot days or long transport we may suggest sturdier options such as ganache or buttercream.

Can you make less‑sweet options?

Yes — we can balance sweetness in buttercreams and fillings within safe recipe limits.

Nut‑free or alcohol‑free?

Possible by request. Please disclose requirements early; see Allergens for more details.

Can you match a theme or colour palette?

Yes. Share references (photos/Pinterest), colours and the vibe — we keep finishes clean and modern.

Logos, characters and fonts

We’re happy to use logos/fonts if you own the rights or have permission. Licensed characters are case‑by‑case; we may decline requests that risk IP infringement.

Will it look exactly like the photo?

Each cake is handcrafted; minor variations happen. Fresh florals/produce depend on season and availability.

Do you do writing or topper names?

Yes — simple name/number toppers available; writing on cake is possible for many designs.

Can you colour‑match to invitations or dresses?

We’ll get close using gel colours/dusts; exact Pantone matches aren’t always feasible in edible media.

How many revisions are included?

Your quote includes one minor revision. Extra concept rounds may be billed.

Do you send a sketch or mock‑up?

For complex tiers, yes — a simple sketch or digital mock‑up can be provided.

Do you cater for allergies?

We bake in a kitchen that handles common allergens. For details, see the Flavours & Allergens section on this page and confirm requirements when ordering.

Do you offer vegan or gluten‑free?

Limited options may be possible without guarantee of no trace. For severe allergies, our products are not suitable.

How do you provide allergen information?

We confirm allergens in writing and ask you to re‑confirm on the order form, aligning with FSA best practice.

Do you avoid specific colourings/additives?

Tell us early; we’ll advise feasibility and alternatives.

Kids’ parties — anything special to note?

Always brief the host on allergens and remove non‑edible supports before serving.

How should I store my cake?

Refrigerate at 0–5 °C; bring to room temperature before serving. For food businesses in England/Wales/NI, cold foods must be kept ≤ 8 °C; fridges are commonly set to 5 °C.

How long will it keep?

Best on the day or next morning; typically up to 3–5 days refrigerated, depending on flavour/filling. Follow our care card.

Heatwave policy

In hot weather we may suggest earlier delivery/collection or sturdier finishes. We cannot accept responsibility for heat damage after handover.

How do I transport it safely?

Carry flat (boot/footwell), air‑con on, away from sun. Drive smoothly; avoid laps/seats. We photograph items before handover for records.

Can you add fresh flowers? Are they food‑safe?

We use food‑safe barriers/picks and avoid toxic species; stems aren’t inserted directly into cake. We follow current UK guidance trends on safe floral use and may decline unsuitable species.

Can I supply my own topper?

Yes — bring it clean, food‑safe and sized to your tier. We can also supply simple name/number toppers.

What non‑edible supports are inside?

Tall/tiered cakes include boards/dowels. Remove all non‑edibles before serving.

When can you refuse or cancel an order?

We may refuse or cancel in cases of abusive behaviour, suspected fraud, unsafe conditions, impossible deadlines or IP/legal concerns. All key decisions are confirmed in writing (email).

Proof and condition at handover

We may photograph your cake before handover as condition proof; after handover, care is the customer’s responsibility.

Heat and storage risks

Once collected or delivered, you’re responsible for cool, flat transport and storage per our care guidance.
